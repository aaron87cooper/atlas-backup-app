Privacy policy

Atlas backup is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers. It asks Google for access only to the files it creates in the owner's own Google Drive (the drive.file scope), and uses that access only to store and read the owner's encrypted backups.

It collects no data, has no server, shares nothing with anyone, and uses no analytics or cookies. Backup contents are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload, so Google stores only encrypted data.

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
